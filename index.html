Frequently asked questions

A few things worth knowing about Zuvnelo.

What is Zuvnelo?

Zuvnelo is a simple workspace for checking, organizing, and saving social-media links.

Can I download videos and audio right now?

Not yet. This version checks links and opens original pages. A compatible download backend has not been connected.

Where are my saved links stored?

Recent links and favourites are stored in local storage in the browser on your device.

Does Zuvnelo need an account?

This version does not require a login or signup.

Your space, your links.

Zuvnelo stores your recent links and favourites locally in this browser. This static version does not send those saved entries to a Zuvnelo server. External websites you open may have their own privacy policies. Do not save sensitive or private links on a shared device.
